Privacy Policy

Animal Sounds (Android app com.star343.animalsounds) · Effective 17 November 2026

Animal Sounds is made for young children (ages 2–5) and their grown-ups. This policy explains what the app does with information. This English version is the official one; the app shows it in English in every language.

We don't collect personal information

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information about children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

The app works offline

The app does not request Android's internet permission and does not send anything over the internet. Links in the grown-ups area (behind a parent gate), such as the sound credits, open in your own web browser.

What stays on the device

  • Quiz progress (which animals were answered correctly, and daily accuracy for the last 30 days), used only to show the weekly progress card in the grown-ups area.
  • Settings: music on/off, sounds on/off, the chosen app language and quiz difficulty.

This information never leaves the device through the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval. Its audio library declares two system permissions used only for playing music smoothly: checking network state and keeping the device awake during playback. Neither gives access to personal data or the internet.

Purchases and ads

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If either is added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any ads will come only from Google Play Families-certified ad providers and never appear on children's screens.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side. Children use the app without typing or sharing anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
